A plain click in the pinned “Tasks” tab on a link to another site. The page under it doesn't move; the card is a live page. Same window and same moment in each option; only the card's look differs. Plan: docs/PEEK-PLAN.md.

A · Arc's layout recommended

Arc's measured spot: the card nearly fills the page's height, a column of round buttons beside it (close, open as tab, split view later). The page behind shrinks a little and dims. The sidebar's address field shows the Peek's host; the pinned tab gets a small badge. No bar on the card: the page gets every point.
