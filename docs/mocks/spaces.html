1. The switcher in the footer

Hover the footer icons. Arc shows the icons, the current one bright.

2. The switch

Swipe slowly to see the in-between frames, or press Slow swipe. The favorites row never moves: it's shared.

3. The Space's colour

Where the colour goes. It cross-fades with the swipe in every option. Press ⌘T for the command bar: tabs from other Spaces show their Space.

4. Creating and editing a Space

Press New Space (or the + in the footer). Right-click a footer icon for Edit Space…, Move Left/Right and Delete Space….
