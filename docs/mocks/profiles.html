Ray Profiles mocks

Profiles

Arc's model: a profile keeps its own logins, cookies, history, passwords, autofill and extensions; Spaces pick a profile, and favorites belong to the profile. Ray already has a profile per Space (Edit Space › Profile). These are the decisions left. Pick one per row (1–5); row 6 is a list to tick.

1. Where Spaces and Profiles live in Settings

The layouts are in settings-spaces.html: A two panes (Spaces, Profiles), B Arc's one pane with Profiles holding their Spaces, C Spaces as pictures with Profiles below. Pick there.

2. Deleting a profile

2A Its Spaces move to Default

The profile's data goes; its Spaces stay, on Default (signed out of what that profile was signed in to). Nothing in the sidebar disappears.

Delete the “Work” profile?

Its passwords, cookies, history and extensions are deleted. Work and Clients move to Default.

2B Its Spaces go with it Arc

Arc's: deleting a profile deletes its Spaces too. Their tabs go to Recently Closed (⇧⌘T brings them back into the Space you're on).

Delete the “Work” profile?

Its Spaces (Work, Clients), and its passwords, cookies, history and extensions are deleted.

2C Ask which

The question offers both; moving to Default is chosen. One more decision at a destructive moment.

Delete the “Work” profile?

Its passwords, cookies, history and extensions are deleted. Its Spaces:

4. Arc's profiles on import

4A Each one a profile, no question

Every Arc profile becomes a Ray profile of the same name; its Spaces point at it; its passwords, cookies, autofill and history go into it. Default stays Default. A re-import finds the same ones.

6. What else Arc's profiles do (tick what you want now)

  • Little Ray opens in the profile of the Space you're on (today always Default), so a link from Slack is signed in as Work while you're in Work.
  • A private window per profile: ⇧⌘N opens the private side of the Space's profile (today always Default's).
  • Command bar: tabs and history from the profile you're on rank first; "Switch to Tab" across profiles keeps working.
  • Extensions per profile in site controls and the toolbar follow the Space (today the first profile used).
  • Settings › Profiles' New Profile… without making a Space (Arc makes them from a Space only).
