Arc extras for Ray

Seven of Arc's smaller pieces, plus downloads and bookmarks moved into the sidebar (section 8) that Ray still lacks (docs/MISSING.md, Arc's list items 8, 13, 15, 17, 18; Ardia's list item 9; and Collapse/Expand All Folders). Each section has 2–3 looks to pick from; the first is the recommended one unless marked otherwise. Sizes are Ray's (267 pt sidebar drawn at 236 here, 41 pt rows at 34, as in the other mocks) and Ardia's theme.ts. Flip Light/Dark to see both.

6. Capture

The page dims; drag an area, click an element, or take the full page (the whole scroll height, through Chrome's Page.captureScreenshot with captureBeyondViewport, an engine call); then Copy or Save. 8. Downloads and bookmarks inside the sidebar

A redo of two things already built: Downloads (⌥⌘L) and Bookmarks (⌥⌘B) open today as Panels popups above their footer buttons. Here they open inside the sidebar, and the footer gets one button that moves: a download flies into it, a ring fills around it, it bounces when the file lands. Nothing changes in the engine: the same ac_downloads_* and ac_bookmarks_* calls, the same rows, menus and search as today's panels, in a new place.

ArcLibrary button at the footer's left. Clicking it slides the whole sidebar sideways to a Library page (Downloads, Media, Easels, Archive), with a back arrow; the tabs come back the same way. A finished download flies into the button. No bookmarks.
ArdiaDownloads as a 300 pt popover with an arrow from its footer button (DownloadsButton, downloadsPlacement); bookmarks on a page of their own.
ChromeThe download bubble from a toolbar icon with a progress ring; bookmarks in the menu, the bar and a side panel.
Ray todayA downloads ring on the footer button (downloadsRing), Downloads and Bookmarks as two buttons and two panels over the sidebar (owner's pick B of bookmarks.html).

The moving button shared by A, B and C

One footer button, "Library", replaces the two. It shows what's happening: at rest, a tray; starting a download, an arrow drops into the tray; while files come in, a ring fills in the profile colour (the ring Ray already has) with a count when there are two or more; when one finishes, a file flies from the page into the button along a curve, the button bounces once on the sidebar's spring, and a tick shows for 1.5 s. A download waiting on Keep/Discard turns the ring orange. Reduce Motion: no flight, no bounce, the ring and tick only. Press Replay to watch it.

The Library: the button grows into the view proposal

Click the footer button and the button itself grows, inside the sidebar: its rounded square stretches out to the sidebar's width and up to a little over half its height, in the colour of the selected tab row, so it reads as the sidebar's own surface, not a popup. As it grows, the tab list above gets shorter to make room (nothing is covered; the last rows fade out at its new edge and it still scrolls). Once the shape is most of the way there, its contents fade in and the rows rise one after another. Closing runs it backwards: the contents fade, the shape folds back into the button, the tabs grow back down.

Motion: shape on the sidebar's spring (0.26 s response, 0.86 damping, about 0.45 s to settle), contents in after 0.12 s, rows 30 ms apart; closing fades contents in 0.1 s first. Reduce Motion: a 0.2 s fade, no growth. Click the button to try it.
