App icons on Neon CRT

Ray's new mark (your pick, Neon CRT from logo-scanline.html) and the themes users can choose instead. Every theme keeps the same R in 16 scanlines, its bloom, the tile's faint scanlines and vignette, so only colour or one effect changes and it's always Ray. Each is an Icon Composer document, Branding/alternates/<name>.icon (Background, Glyph, a white TintedGlyph for Clear and Tinted, and Decor on themed ones), made by tools/alternate-icons.py. Mocks only: nothing here is built.

How the pick would stick (from the local session's research): Ardia, Circa and Crest keep a chosen icon with a Dock tile plug-in, so the icon survives quitting and Sparkle updates without rewriting the signed bundle.

The variants

Each theme at Dock size (128 px) and at 32 px (the app switcher's small size and Finder's list), on a light and a dark desktop.

Themed

Places and seasons around the same R: a neon beach city, a block world, the beach, Christmas, Halloween, space and spring. Original art in each mood, never a game's name, logo or look. The seasonal ones could switch themselves on for their dates later.

Theme colours

One icon per colour Settings › Theme offers (Ray's lavender, then ProfileColor.presets), the colours Spaces will take from Arc. Later the icon could follow the theme colour by itself.

macOS looks

macOS 26 shows every icon in the look the user picks in System Settings › Appearance: Default, Dark, Clear (light or dark) and Tinted. Each icon is an Icon Composer document (Branding/alternates/<name>.icon) with a white one-colour glyph for Clear and Tinted, so the R stays crisp on glass. Light tiles (Paper, Beach, Blocks, Sakura, Classic) give way to macOS's dark fill in Dark. Clear and Tinted here are drawn the way macOS draws them; the real thing comes from macOS itself.

Settings › Appearance › App icon

The row is already in the catalog, unbuilt (appIcon, Theme group, rare). Both layouts use PictureChoiceControl's card as it is: a 120×76 picture, a 2.5 pt ring 1.5 pt clear of it in the profile colour, and a check before the caption. Click the pictures.

A · One strip, every icon

  • The control as it is today, one row of cards, scrolling sideways inside the card (24 icons); the chosen one is scrolled into view when the pane opens.
  • Each picture is the icon on a slice of desktop (light or dark with Ray), so Clear shows what it does.
  • Simple to build; long, and the eight colours crowd out the themes.

B · Styles, then a colour

  • Seventeen cards, four to a row: the nine styles, the seven themed icons and Colour. Choosing Colour shows one more line, Icon colour, with Settings' swatches; the first follows the theme colour above.
  • Pictures on the card's band, the icon at 56 pt; Clear sits on a slice of desktop so the glass reads.
  • Needs PictureChoiceControl to wrap (four per row in the 600 pt column) and one extra row; the Spaces colours stay one choice.
