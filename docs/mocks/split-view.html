Split View

Arc's Split View: two to four pages side by side in one window, kept as one row in the sidebar. The scene is a real-size Ray window (sidebar 267 pt, the page card 8 pt from the window's edges with 10 pt corners), scaled to fit. Everything in it works: drag a Today tab onto the page (or onto another row's place), drag the dividers, click a pane or a pill to focus it, right-click rows and pills, ⌘-click two to four tabs and right-click them, and use ⌃⇧= and ⌃⇧− while the scene has the pointer over it. Pick one option per row; the scene combines them.

Drag a Today tab onto the page.

Sidebar row

A split is one row in Today (or Pinned) wherever its first page was. It moves, pins and closes as one, and ⌃Tab, the command bar and Recently Closed treat it as one place with its pages.

A · Pills Arc

The row holds one 35 pt pill per page, 3 pt apart, each with favicon and title (12.5 pt); with 3 or 4 pages only the favicons. The focused page's pill takes the selected row's fill. Click a pill to focus that pane; its ✕ closes that pane. Arc's and Ardia's.

B · Favicons

A normal 41 pt row: the favicons joined by Ray's grey slash (the one rows already use), then the focused page's title at 14 pt. The focused favicon is solid with a small line under it, the others faded. Click a favicon to focus. Calmest; titles of the other pages only on hover (tooltip).

C · Stack

Favicons overlapping like a stack, then the pages' names ("GitHub + Linear", "GitHub + 2"). Reads as "these go together"; focus shows only by which favicon is on top. Clicking the row focuses the pane you used last.

Making a split

Three ways, all options: drag a tab onto the page; ⌃⇧= (Arc's Add Split View) opens an empty pane on the right with the command bar in it, or adds one to a split up to four; the row menu's Add Split View puts that tab beside the current page, and with two to four tabs ⌘-clicked it reads Open 3 Tabs in Split View. Up to two pages sit side by side at 50 : 50; three share the width; four make a 2×2 grid (220 pt is the narrowest a pane gets, Arc's). A fifth is refused with a note ("Split View holds up to 4 pages"). Dropping a tab in the sidebar right between a split's pills (option A) adds it there too.

A · Side cards Arc

As the dragged tab reaches the page, two frosted cards with a dashed edge rise at its left and right ("Add Left Split", "Add Right Split"); the one on the pointer's side grows, leans toward it and turns the profile colour. Sizes and stages are Ardia's measurements of Arc (13 %→22 %→24 % of the page wide).

B · Half preview

The page itself shows where the tab would land: the side it takes fills with the profile colour's wash and the tab's favicon and title, the rest shrinks to make room. What you see is what you get; no labels.

Dividers and resizing

A · Arc's gap Arc

Panes are separate cards 10 pt apart on the sidebar colour; the gap is the handle (resize cursor across it). Hovering shows a 2×22 pt grip in its middle. Live resize, one snap at equal sizes (within 10 pt), 220 pt minimum, double click does nothing. The sizes are kept per split. Measured by Open Arc.

B · Thin, with ratio

A 6 pt gap, so the pages get 4 pt more. Hovering lights a 2 pt line in the profile colour; dragging shows the split as a ratio ("62 : 38") that snaps to 50 : 50, and a double click evens the panes out.

Pane top

A · Bar per pane Arc

Every pane has its own 32 pt bar: back and forward, the host centred on a pill, ⋯ (pane menu) and ✕. The unfocused panes' bars at half strength until hovered. Arc's; costs 32 pt of each page. The sidebar address field shows the focused pane.

B · On hover

No bar: pages use the full height. The pointer near a pane's top brings in a small frosted capsule (Ray's panel surface): favicon, host, ⋯ and ✕. Back, forward and the address are the sidebar's (or the page address bar's), for the focused pane.

Which pane has focus

Focus follows a click in a pane, a click on its pill or favicon, and ⌃Tab within the split (question 3). Back, forward, reload, ⌘L, ⌘F, zoom and the sidebar's address field act on the focused pane; ⌘W closes it.

A · Ring Ardia

A 2 pt ring in the profile colour 1 pt outside the focused pane (Ardia's), plus the sidebar's mark. Always clear; a little loud.

B · Quiet Arc

Nothing on the page: the focused pane's bar is full strength (option A of Pane top), and the sidebar marks it. Arc's. With Pane top B the page shows no focus at all.

C · Dim the others

The other panes take a faint scrim (7 % light, 28 % dark) that lifts under the pointer. Clear without a coloured edge, but dims what you might be reading.

Closing a pane and leaving the split

The pane's ✕, its pill's ✕, ⌃⇧− (Arc's Close this Split Pane) and ⌘W close the focused pane. When one page is left the row becomes an ordinary tab. Separate All (the split row's menu) gives each page its own row again, the focused one active; Separate “Linear” from Split View does that for one pane; Close Split View closes all of them, and ⇧⌘T brings back the whole split.

A · Closes the tab Arc

✕ closes that page's tab, as Arc does: it goes to Recently Closed and ⇧⌘T puts it back in its pane.

B · Moves it out

✕ takes the page out of the split as its own row right after it (nothing closes), with a short "Moved Out of Split View" note and Undo. ⌘W and ⌃⇧− still close.

Questions for you

  1. Vertical splits: Arc and Open Arc allow top/bottom splits (and Circa's Convert to Vertical). Only the 2×2 grid for four here: add a Convert to Vertical item, or leave it out?
  2. Pinned: a split in Pinned keeps its pinned pages and sizes across launches (Arc's). OK, and a split of favorites stays out (a favorite dragged onto the page opens beside it as a Today tab)?
  3. Keys inside a split: ⌃Tab walks every tab (a split counts once); for moving focus between panes add ⌃⇧[ and ⌃⇧] (Circa's Focus Previous/Next Split Pane), or none?
  4. Command bar in a pane: ⌘T while a split is shown opens a new tab (Arc's), not a new pane; ⌘L edits the focused pane. OK?
