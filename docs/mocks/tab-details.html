Tab details

Three small things Chrome and Arc have: the link's address on hover, a sound icon on tabs, and the back/forward history list. Pick a look for each.

1. Link address on hover

Hover the links. Then move the pointer into the dashed corner: the address moves to the other corner.

  • Chrome's timing: shows 80 ms after the pointer reaches a link (120 ms fade), waits 250 ms after it leaves, then fades over 200 ms; moving between links swaps the text with no fade.
  • Pill A is Ardia's size: 6 pt in, 22 pt tall, 7 pt corners, 12 pt text, on the notes' surface. B is Chrome's corner tab.
  • At most a third of the page wide (Chrome's); after 1.6 s still on a link, a cut address grows to the page's width.
  • Host in medium weight, the rest grey, cut from the end; the host is kept whole. user:pass@ hidden, percent-encoding kept (punycode hosts, as the address bar). javascript: shows as just that word.
  • Moving aside: Chrome slides its bubble down off the page as the pointer nears; Arc and this mock jump to the other corner. Ardia, Crest and Cra don't move at all.
  • Never shown while the command bar, find bar or a panel covers that corner. Over the page's own pixels only, below the sidebar peek.

3. Back / forward history list

Right-click back or forward, or press and hold (0.5 s). Choose a page to go there. Try both the page bar and the sidebar's buttons.

  • Newest step first (the page you'd reach with one click on top), at most 12, then "Show Full History" (⌘Y), as Chrome's list (no "Show Full History" in private windows). Panel 300 pt wide, 32 pt rows (Ardia's).
  • Titles from the engine's history entries; the favicon from Chrome's favicon store (an entry without one gets a globe). A page with no title shows its address.
  • The panel opens from the button on the panels' spring, below the button (over the sidebar for the sidebar's buttons); ↑ ↓ ⏎ and Escape work. ⌘-click a row opens it in a new tab.
  • Hold: the list opens after 0.5 s with the button still down (Chrome's 500 ms; Ardia 450 ms), or at once on a drag down; a quick click still goes back. Crest has right-click only, Cra none. Out of scope: Dia's two-finger swipe-and-hold list (noted for later).
