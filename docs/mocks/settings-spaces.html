Spaces and Profiles in Settings

The sidebar keeps New Space, Edit Space (the 4B panel), the footer icons and the swipe. Settings is where you see all your Spaces and Profiles at once, rename, reorder, re-theme, delete, and manage profiles and their data. Click rows to open them. Pick one option, or say what to mix.

A. Two panes: Spaces, and Profiles

A Spaces pane + Profiles pane

Spaces: one card, a row per Space (drag the grip to reorder), opening to its name, icon, Window and Accent, profile, and Delete Space…. Profiles: a row per profile with its Spaces under it, opening to rename, colour, Delete Profile… (deletes its cookies, history, passwords and extensions).

B. One pane, Arc's: Profiles holding their Spaces

B Spaces and Profiles Arc

Arc's Settings › Profiles: each profile is a group, its Spaces are its rows. Moving a Space to another profile is its row's Profile pop-up. New Profile in the pane's header; a profile's ⋯ renames, recolours or deletes it.

C. One pane with pictures

C Spaces as pictures, Profiles below

Each Space a small picture of the window in its theme (Settings' live-picture look); click one to edit it in the card below. Profiles as a plain list underneath.

Either way

  1. The data panes (Passwords, Autofill, Extensions, Privacy) show the profile of the Space you're on, with a profile pop-up at the top when you have more than one (asked next, separately).
  2. Deleting a profile asks first, says which Spaces move to Default, and deletes its data from disk.
  3. ray://settings/spaces and ray://settings/profiles open them; the command bar finds them ("Settings: Spaces").
