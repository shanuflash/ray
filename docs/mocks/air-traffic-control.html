Air Traffic Control

Arc's Air Traffic Control: routes that send links opened from other apps (Slack, Mail, Notes) to a chosen Space or to Little Ray. A route matches by site, by what the address contains or by the exact address; the first route that matches wins, and links no route takes go to the default. Links clicked inside Ray never go through it. It lives in a new Settings pane, Links, which also takes General's Little Ray switch. Drawn as Ray's Settings window at full size (880×600), scaled to fit; everything in it works: add, edit, reorder (drag the handle in A), remove, change the default and paste links into the tester. Pick one option per row.

Route list

A · Arc's sentences Arc

Each route reads as Arc's sheet does: "If URL [Contains ▾] [github.com]" over "↳ Open in [Work ▾]", with a handle to drag it up or down and ⊖ to remove it at once. Everything is edited in place and saved as you type; New Route adds an empty one at the end (Contains, Most Recent Space). Arc's words.

B · Ray's rows

Settings' own data list (Privacy's, Passwords'): one 44 pt row per route, the Space's icon, what it matches as the title and "Site · opens in Work" under it. A row opens to a small form under it (EditorFields, Delete in red letters, Cancel and Save); its "…" moves it up or down. New Route opens the form for a new one. Calmer with many routes; one more click to edit.

Match

A · Pick the kind

A pop-up of three: Site is (the host and its subdomains: github.com takes gist.github.com), Contains (anywhere in the address, Arc's), Is equal to (the whole address, Arc's; a trailing slash and http/https don't count). Arc has the last two; Ardia and Cra add the site match, which is what people mean most of the time. New routes start on Site is.

B · Guess from the text

No pop-up: one field, and a chip at its end says how it reads what you typed. A bare host (figma.com) is a Site, text with a path (google.com/document) Contains, a whole address starting with https:// Is equal to. Click the chip to pick another. Fewer controls; the chip keeps it honest.

Default

A · Last row Arc

Arc's: the routes card ends in "Default ↳ Open in [Most Recent Space ▾]", what every other link does. The Little Ray switch stays above as its own row; choosing Little Ray as the default turns it on, any other choice turns it off (one setting underneath).

B · First question

The pane opens with the one question most people need: "Open links from other apps in [Little Ray ▾]" (Little Ray, Most Recent Space, or a Space). It replaces the switch; the routes below are the exceptions to it. One control instead of a switch and a row that move together.

Try a link

A · One answer

Paste or type a link and the answer shows at once: where it would open and which route sent it ("Route 2: Site is figma.com", or "No route: the default"). The route that took it gets a mark on its left edge and flashes as Settings search results do.

B · The route it took

The same field, answered as the path the link takes: each route in order, crossed off until one matches (the ones after it greyed: never checked), then where it lands. Shows why "first match wins" matters when two routes overlap.

Behaviour (both looks)

  • Only links that other apps hand to Ray (LinksFromApps) are routed; links inside Ray, the command bar and bookmarks never are. Files from Finder aren't either.
  • A Space destination opens the link as a Today tab in that Space, in the front window that shows it (switching that window to it), else the front window; Most Recent Space is the front window's Space; Little Ray opens it there (and Little Ray's Open in Ray goes to the front window's Space). Spaces of another profile show "(Profile)" after the name once Profiles land.
  • A route whose Space was deleted shows "Deleted Space" in its pop-up and is skipped (its row says so in red) until you pick another; a route with nothing typed matches nothing.
  • Matching ignores case, and the scheme and www. for Site is. Saved in Settings.json as a list (linkRoutes), like the site searches, with the default as linkDefault.
