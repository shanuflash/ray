Same in every option, unless you say otherwise

  • Range 240–480 pt, default 267 (today's width). Under 240 the top row's buttons meet. The page always keeps at least 400 pt, so a narrow window lowers the maximum. Ardia: 160–400. Crest: 200–380. Cra: 220–520.
  • No snapping. The width moves in whole points (Chrome's page needs that). None of the four snaps.
  • One width for every window, remembered across launches (Ardia, Cra). Other windows follow when you let go. Private windows use it and can be dragged, but never save theirs. Crest keeps one per window.
  • Double-click the edge: back to 267, on the sidebar's spring (Ardia). Crest and Cra do nothing.
  • Hidden sidebar (⌘S): the peeking sidebar comes out at the same width. Its edge isn't draggable while it peeks (Cra's way); dock it to resize. Owner, 2026-11-12: it drags while peeking too.
  • "Hide past the minimum" (row 3 above): keep dragging 60 pt past 240 and the sidebar hides, as ⌘S. I remember Arc doing this but couldn't measure it; it's off in my pick because it's easy to trigger by accident.
  • Hit zone: 8 pt, 6 inside the sidebar's empty gutter and 2 over the page's edge ("Show hit zone" above). Ardia 4 pt, Cra 8 pt, Crest 16 pt.
  • No keyboard or VoiceOver resize in this round.
