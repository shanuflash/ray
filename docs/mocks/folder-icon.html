A better folder icon

Today Ray uses macOS's plain folder glyph, an outline when open and filled when closed, the way Arc draws it. The new one is drawn in layers: a back panel with a tab, two sheets of paper and a front flap. Opening, the sheets rise up out of it on a spring, and it takes the folder's colour plus an optional symbol or emoji on its front. Same icon in Pinned and Today.

References: Arc and Ray now draw macOS's glyph, with no picker. Ardia lets a group take an emoji or an SF Symbol from a picker panel. Cra only picks a white or black glyph. Crest offers colour only.

States (4× here, 18 × 16 pt in the sidebar)

Opening: two sheets rise up out of the folder, fanning apart, and the flap leans back, in 0.36 s with a little overshoot. Hover lifts the sheets a touch. While a tab is dragged over the folder it opens wider and pulses. With Reduce Motion it switches without moving.

Customise

Colour: none plus Chrome's eight, as in the folders mock. Icon: none, a symbol (white on the flap, black on yellow so it stays legible) or an emoji. Pick one below to see it on the folder.

In the sidebar (click the folders)

Edit Folder (in the sidebar, no popover)

Right-click a folder › Edit Folder… (or New Folder) opens this under its row: name, colour, and icon with Symbols and Emoji (Ardia's lists, searchable). Return or a click elsewhere keeps it, Escape undoes it. Colour ▸ stays in the menu as a shortcut.

Notes for the build

DrawingCALayers: a back shape layer, two paper layers and a front layer with its anchor at the bottom edge, The sheets' rise and the flap's lean run on CASpringAnimations. Nothing runs on a main-thread timer.
ColourThe flap is the colour with a light top edge; the back is a shade darker. With no colour it's a neutral grey that suits both light and dark.
GlyphSF Symbol at 7.5 pt semibold, in the white or black that reads better on the colour (ProfileColor.foreground(on:)); or an emoji. Saved with the folder in Sidebar.json.
Today foldersChrome's group has only a title and colour: the icon is Ray's own, kept in Sidebar.json.
MenuRename… becomes Edit Folder… (name, colour and icon in one place); Colour ▸ stays.
