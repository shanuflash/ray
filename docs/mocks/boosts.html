Boosts

Arc's Boosts for Ray: change a site's colours, font, text size and case, hide parts of it with Zap, and, for those who want it, your own CSS and JavaScript. A Boost belongs to one site (made from the tab's verified origin) and never runs anywhere else; JavaScript is off until you turn it on for that Boost. The plan, the engine options and the questions are in docs/BOOSTS-PLAN.md. Pick one option per row (click a card); the scenes are drawn at Ray's sizes and scaled to fit. The site is a made-up news page, The Daily Tide.
